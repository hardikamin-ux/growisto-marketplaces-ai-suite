Amazon Listing Generator

Claude Plugin — Follow these steps to get started

1
Open Claude at claude.ai and sign in with your Growisto account.
2
Navigate to Plugins from the left sidebar or settings menu.
3
Search for and install Growisto Amazon Listing Generator.
4
Start a new chat and type /amazon-listing-generator to launch the 9-step listing workflow.
5
Follow the prompts — select marketplace, browse node, tone, add product details, and receive your fully formatted listing with an Excel export.
This plugin runs entirely inside Claude. No external login or setup required beyond the one-time install.

3
