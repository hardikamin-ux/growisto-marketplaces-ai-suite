Claude Plugin

Amazon Review Sentiment Analysis

Turn raw Amazon review exports into a decision-ready Excel workbook in minutes. A four-stage pipeline — cleaning, AI tagging, aggregation, and delivery — produces four structured sheets covering headline KPIs, ranked problem areas, rating trends, and a feature satisfaction matrix.

How to Use
1
Open Claude Go to claude.ai and sign in with your Growisto account.
2
Go to Plugins Navigate to Plugins from the left sidebar or settings menu.
3
Install the Plugin Search for and install Growisto VOC Review.
4
Upload Your Review File Start a new chat, upload your Amazon review export file, and type /voc-review to begin analysis.
5
Receive Your Report The tool processes your reviews through a four-stage pipeline and delivers a branded four-sheet Excel workbook.
What You Get
Summary Sheet Headline KPIs, average rating, verified purchase rate, cohort split, top 5 problems, and an auto-generated recommendation.
Problem Areas Recurring issues ranked by frequency with colour-coded urgency flags — red, amber, and green — for instant prioritisation.
Rating Trend Month-by-month breakdown of critical vs. positive review ratios with spike flags and recent trajectory indicators.
Feature Satisfaction Matrix Aspect-by-aspect sentiment scoring across quality, durability, sizing, delivery, and more — with a satisfaction index per feature.
This plugin runs entirely inside Claude. Your review data stays in your session and is never stored externally.
